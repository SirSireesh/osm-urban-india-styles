### Styles

#### Urban

Fork of [OpenFreeMap's Liberty style](https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles), which is a fork of [maputnik/osm-liberty](https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty). OSM Liberty is a fork of OSM Bright. The OSM Bright GL Style is derived from [Mapbox Open Styles](https://github.com/mapbox/mapbox-gl-styles). The style code is released under the [BSD 3-Clause License](<https://tldrlegal.com/license/bsd-3-clause-license-(revised)>). The style design is released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [LICENSE.md](styles/urban/LICENSE.md).
